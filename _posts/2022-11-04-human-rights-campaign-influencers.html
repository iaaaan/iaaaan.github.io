--- title: "The information void and the people who fill it" date: 2022-11-04 00:00:00 -0500 categories: human-rights-campaign anchor: influencers type: process ---

Progressive media had failed to invest in this issue, and the information void had been filled by anti-trans influencers, who were setting the agenda. I set out to find out who they were.

I found that in 2021, a staggering 95% of retweets on anti-trans articles could be traced back to posts by a coordinated group of 20 activists.

On the other hand, noticeably absent from the conversation, was the much larger and otherwise most vocal group on Twitter: Gen Z. This demographic group generally favored other social media platforms over Twitter, but even on this platform it had previously been a dominant force in the trans rights discourse. In this case, Gen Z was the group least represented, and a time-based analysis of demographics in the conversation revealed how older generated had taken over.

The absence of kids in this conversation about trans kids, combined with the dominance of a few political actors, was the demonstration of a coordinated, astroturfed effort to manipulate the public opinion. It never was about fairness in sports: it was another attempt to erase trans people from public life, similar to previous moral panics.
